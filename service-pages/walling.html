Walling

Solid, expertly crafted garden walls built to add structure, define boundaries and give your outdoor space lasting character.

Structure That Defines Your Space

Walls That Work Hard and Look Beautiful

Garden walls serve both a practical and aesthetic purpose. A well-built wall can retain a sloped garden, define outdoor rooms, create raised beds or simply provide a beautiful boundary that frames your space.

Our skilled bricklayers work with natural stone, brick, block and rendered finishes to create walls that are structurally sound and visually impressive. We handle everything from footings and drainage to the final capping and pointing.

Whether you need a low decorative border, a full boundary wall or a substantial retaining structure, we assess the ground conditions and loads involved to build something that will stand strong for decades.
